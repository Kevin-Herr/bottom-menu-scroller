Glass Dock

A floating menu that starts as one button. Put it anywhere, then tap to open it.

  • Drag the buttonPlace it anywhere. Flick it and it glides to a stop.
  • Tap to openThe menu grows out of the button and docks at the bottom.
  • Swipe left or rightBrowse the icons in the current menu.
  • Swipe up or downCycle through Everyday, Create, Listen & Watch, and Controls.
  • Keyboard & trackpadArrow keys move and switch menus, Esc closes. A scroll wheel or trackpad works too.
